PROJECT OVERVIEW

ABOUT THE PROJECT

A handmade engineering model that turns an idea into a tangible, presentable prototype.

This student project explores the visual and structural language of modern defence vehicles using cardboard and accessible materials. The focus is on structural design, creative engineering, realistic detailing, teamwork and a remote-control concept — not on creating an operational weapon.

01STRUCTURAL DESIGNLayered body, chassis and launcher-support geometry.
02CREATIVE ENGINEERINGPractical problem-solving with accessible materials.
03TEAMWORKPlanning, assembly, colouring and detailing as a group.
04REMOTE-CONTROL CONCEPTAn educational control-system idea for the model.

DESIGN & COLOUR

A restrained palette keeps the model technical, readable and exhibition-ready.
